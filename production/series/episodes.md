## Episode-by-episode tonal arc

```
Ep 1  ──────  awe + dread
Ep 2  ──────  awe + dread → tenderness (Tomasz)
Ep 3  ──────  tenderness + grief (Morrow) → joy (June)
Ep 4  ──────  political velocity, comic intervals
Ep 5  ──────  grief at scale + climax of pain → witness
Ep 6  ──────  acceptance + farewell + the strange morning
```

The shape of the season is a held breath that becomes a long exhale. Episodes 1 and 2 are the inhale. Episode 3 is the chest holding still. Episodes 4 and 5 are the necessary tension before release. Episode 6 is the breath leaving. Hold for one beat after Episode 6 ends. Then credits.

## Per-episode review gates

For every episode, six approvals are required from the producer (you):

1. **Synopsis** — does the heart feel right?
2. **Outline** — do the beats land?
3. **Script** — do the lines work in your mouth?
4. **Shotlist** — is this the cheapest, clearest way to shoot it?
5. **Animatic** — does it play? **(largest gate)**
6. **Final cut** — sign off for delivery.

Six gates × six episodes = 36 approvals across the season. The dashboard tracks each one.
